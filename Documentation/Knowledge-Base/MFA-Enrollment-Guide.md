# How-To Guide: Setting Up Your Microsoft Authenticator App

* **Target Audience:** All Company Employees & New Hires
* **Estimated Setup Time:** 5-7 minutes
* **What You Need:** Your temporary corporate login credentials, your daily smartphone (iOS or Android), and computer.

---

## Quick Summary
Setting up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is a mandatory step to keep our corporate network and your personal account secure from unauthorized access. We use the Microsoft Authenticator app because push notifications are far safer and faster than text messages.

Have your phone and temporary password ready? Let's walk through it together.

---

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Head to Your Security Portal
1. Open up Chrome, Safari, or whatever browser you prefer on your computer.
2. Type [https://myprofile.microsoft.com](https://myprofile.microsoft.com) into the search bar.
3. Sign in using the temporary corporate email and password provided by IT.
4. When a screen pops up saying **"More information required"**, go ahead and click **Next**

### Step 2: Install the App on Your Phone
1. Open the **App Store** (if you're on an iPhone)  or the **Google Play Store** (if you're on an Android)
2. Search for **Microsoft Authenticator** and download it.
3. *Quick Tip:* Try to skip SMS verification if the prompt gives you a choice. App-based push notifications are much more reliable and secure.

### Step 3: Link Your work Account
1. Back on your computer monitor, choose **Authenticator App** from the dropdown options and hit **Next**.
2. A large **QR code** should now be showing on your desktop screen.
3. Open the Authenticator app on your phone, tap the **"+" (plus)** icon in the top corner to add a new account, and choose **Work or school account**.
4. Point your phone's camera at the computer monitor to scan the QR code.

### Step 4: Complete Connection
1. Once your phone scans the code, your work account will instantly show up inside the mobile app.
2. Click **Next** on your computer screen.
3. Tap **Approve** on your phone, and you're officially all setup!

## Common Issues & Quick Fixes
* **"My camera won't scan the QR code!"**
  * *The Fix:* This usually happens if your screen is too dim. Try turning up your computer display brightness. Still stuck? Click the **"Can't scan image"** link right below the QR code to manually type in the secret key and URL.
* **"I just upgraded my phone / got locked out of my device"**
  * *The Fix:* No worries! Send a quick email to the IT Helpdesk at helpdesk@company.com; An administrator will need to temporarily clear your old device token so you can re-enroll your new phone.
